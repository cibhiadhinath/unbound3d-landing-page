How It Works

A disciplined path to finished parts

We eliminate the coordination overhead of working across multiple machine shops and suppliers.

1. Requirement & DFM Review

We review your CAD files (STEP, IGES, STL, DXF) and 2D PDF drawings for manufacturability, tolerances, and material feasibility before quoting.

2. Process Selection & Quote

Receive an engineer-reviewed quote with transparent lead times, process recommendations, and commercial terms within 6 business hours.

3. Manufacturing & QC

Parts are manufactured in-house or through qualified partner facilities under strict process control and intermediate quality checkpoints.

4. Delivery with Inspection Report

Finished components arrive at your facility with dimensional inspection reports, secure packaging, and full batch traceability.
