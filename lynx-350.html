A production platform, not just a printer.

The Lynx-350 is a production-grade system engineered for high-performance thermoplastics and reinforced composites. It features an integrated material conditioning workflow, a stable thermal environment, and consistent dimensional accuracy for batch-to-batch repeatability.

Thermal Control System

350°C Extruder & 150°C Bed

A stable thermal environment is critical for engineering polymers. Our 70°C actively heated chamber (forced convection) reduces warping and promotes consistent layer adhesion for materials like PA-CF and PPS-CF.

Structural Rigidity & Motion

40,000 mm/s² Acceleration

Built on a massive rigid frame, the Lynx-350 delivers high-speed printing up to 650 mm/s while maintaining part tolerances. The triple independent Z-axis drives an automatically levelled bed under heavy build loads.

Precision Calibration

Non-contact eddy current scanning

Fast, repeatable bed calibration in under 60 seconds without manual adjustments or paper feeler tests. Built for shop-floor production readiness.

Twin Dry Material Conditioning

Dual spool system with active heated drying up to 110°C.

Atmospheric moisture degrades the mechanical integrity of polyamides and reinforced filaments. The Twin Dry system actively dries and conditions your filament during printing, ensuring consistent mechanical properties, seamless spool changeover, and clean surface finishes.

Engineered for Advanced Materials

With an extruder rated to 350°C and a 70°C actively heated chamber (forced convection), the Lynx-350 is built to process structural composite filaments and durable engineering polymers.
