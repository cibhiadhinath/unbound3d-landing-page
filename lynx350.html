Engineering

A production platform, not just a printer

An 80 °C active chamber and integrated Twin Dry material conditioning deliver consistent results from batch to batch.

Thermal control

450 °C nozzle, 150 °C bed, 80 °C active chamber. Thermal stability is critical for engineering polymers. The heated chamber reduces warping and improves layer adhesion for materials like PA-CF and PPS-CF.

Rigidity & motion

650 mm/s, 40,000 mm/s². A rigid CoreXY frame with triple independent Z keeps the bed trammed.

Calibration

Eddy-current scanning probe. Non-contact bed scanning for repeatable calibration without manual tuning.

Material conditioning

Twin Dry

A heated dual-spool dryer, up to 110 °C.

Moisture degrades engineering-grade filaments. Twin Dry conditions the material while you print, for reliable mechanical properties and a better surface finish.
