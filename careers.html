Join Unbound3D

Build physical hardware that matters.

We are a Bengaluru-based hardware and manufacturing company building industrial machines and delivering critical parts for aerospace, defence, robotics, and EV engineering teams across India.

Why engineers join Unbound3D

At Unbound3D, you do not sit in endless sprint planning meetings talking about mockups. You design mechanical systems, write motion control firmware, evaluate GD&T on real CMM reports, and see parts come off CNC machines and 3D printer beds every single day.

We value first-principles reasoning, high ownership, direct communication, and a genuine respect for manufacturing craftsmanship.
