Execution Flow

How a job moves through Unbound3D

Every job follows a verified sequence to eliminate production surprises.

01

File Upload & Intake

Submit your 3D CAD files (STEP, IGES, STL) and 2D drawings (PDF, DXF). We review all notes, critical dimensions, fit requirements, and intended operating environments.

02

DFM & Manufacturability Review

An applications engineer evaluates your geometry for tool access, wall thicknesses, corner radii, draft angles, and raw material availability. If we spot a risk or cost driver, we suggest practical adjustments before manufacturing begins.

03

Clear Quoting & Process Routing

Within 6 business hours, you receive a detailed quote with itemised pricing, selected manufacturing processes, committed delivery timelines, and finish specifications.

04

Production Management

Jobs are assigned either to our in-house machines or to qualified, vetted partner facilities specialised in specific processes. An Unbound3D manufacturing engineer manages the machine setup, tooling, and intermediate in-process checks.

05

Quality Control & Inspection

Every finished part undergoes dimensional verification using calibrated instruments (micrometres, height gauges, optical comparators) against the customer’s drawing. Threads and critical bores are checked with plug and thread gauges.

06

Protective Dispatch & Documentation

Components are cleaned, deburred, corrosion-protected (if ferrous), and individually packaged for safe transit. Dimensional inspection reports and tracking details are shared directly upon dispatch.
